# The user's look

Step 7 of the run, in full. The lead reads this when the user's notes arrive after a where-to-look post: annotations on localhost, comments on the close issue or a PR, or a reply in chat. Filing follows the SKILL's Keeping the tracker true (assignee, status, priority, effort and type labels, the Design Needed test).

## Reading and filing the notes

- **Read everything pending.** Read every pending Agentation annotation over the MCP, never only the notes the user pasted, and any issue or PR comments.
- **Take a position, then file in the same turn.** Take a position on each in chat, and file the agreed ones in the tracker in the same turn, before any investigation finishes. One you disagree with gets the reason in chat and on the annotation, and stays open until he answers. Once the run has ended, file into the project's improvements project (SKILL, Filing). Batching conceptually is fine; a gap between agreeing and filing isn't.
- **Ordinary issues.** The agreed notes become ordinary issues in the milestone that picks them up, grouped by surface like any others, plus a Design Needed issue for anything that needs a design loop. The run picks them up in order; resolve the annotations when they merge.
- **Design questions** the look raises become an open question in the product doc and an issue labeled Design Needed in the milestone that can take them.

## Nothing waits on his look

- He looks when he wants. No issue, merge or milestone waits on it, and the lead never asks him to look sooner.
- An issue filed from his notes goes to Done on merge like any other, and he sees it at his next look, never in a recheck of its own unless he asks for one.
