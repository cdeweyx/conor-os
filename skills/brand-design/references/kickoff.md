# Kickoff

The work done before the first question, so the interview is "which of these" rather than "what do you like". From Primary Studio's brand sprint, where idea zones built before the call are why most clients approve a direction on the spot.

## Pre-fill

Answer each question from the product doc, DESIGN.md, and the inspection before asking it, and mark a guess where the docs are silent:
- Why it exists and for whom.
- What would feel generic here.
- What physical object or world it could borrow from (a weather map, a receipt, a ticket).
- Three to five competitors and one anti-reference.
- What's fixed.

## The grid

One frame at the top of the brand file's "🚧 Directions" page: the user's references (anything they've shared, plus the liked worlds in `preferences.md`) beside the three to five competitors' marks, sites, and product screens, each labeled. With no user references yet, show the competitors and ask for references in the brand half.

## Zones

Four to six, beside the grid. Each is a named idea taken from the product's own objects (the thing on its screens, the world it could borrow from), never a style word, with six to ten found images. Search wider than you show, then cut until every image carries the idea; a zone that can't keep six is dropped or merged. Check each against "Done before" in `preferences.md` and say when a zone leans on a past move.

## The interview

At most five questions, one at a time, each with its assumed answer so a reply can be "yes".
- **Business half.** Why it exists and for whom, what would feel generic, what's fixed: confirm or correct the pre-fill.
- **Brand half, over the grid and zones.** Which zones carry it, which references, and why; whether the competitors and anti-reference are the right ones.

For every reference the user supplied, write one line in the user's words on what they like about it; ask when they haven't said. An odd reference is often where the direction hides. The line sits with the reference's annotation in step 4.

## The summary (call 1)

One message: every answer, the chosen zones, the why lines, and the menu with the agent's ticks (SKILL.md "The menu"), for one yes.
