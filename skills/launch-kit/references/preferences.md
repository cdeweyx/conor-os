# Preferences

The user's picks for launch assets. Read before intake; add to it at fold back.

- **Voice-over: synthetic by default.** Kokoro locally; ElevenLabs when the voice has to carry the brand. A human read only when the user asks.
- **Engine: HyperFrames by default**, Remotion when the product is React and 3D or captions dominate.
- **Scope: no decks, changelog design, or docs-site look.** Those were cut from the kit; landing-page and brand-design own the OG card, favicon set, and press page.
