# Rubric

What the two blind critics score at the gate. They see two things only: the first three seconds of the master as a strip of frames (every 0.25s), and the Product Hunt thumbnail plus gallery image one at real size beside two competitors'. No brief, no story file, no prior scores.

## Scores, 1 to 10

- **Stop.** Would this frame stop a thumb in a feed with the sound off? The first frame has to read on its own.
- **Point.** From the three seconds alone, can you say what the product does in one sentence? Write the sentence. The lead compares it to the story file's one point; a miss caps Point at 5.
- **Product.** Is the actual product on screen, doing something, by second three? A logo, a gradient, or a title card caps Product at 4.
- **Craft.** Type, color, and motion read as one system with the product's brand, nothing jittery, nothing clipped, captions legible at phone size.
- **Thumbnail.** At 240 wide beside the competitors, which one would you click, and why? Name what you can read.

Median of each across the two critics. Taste overall is the median of the five.

## Tells

Each one present costs a point on Craft, and any two together cap Craft at 6.

- A logo sting or brand intro before the product.
- Kinetic type on every line, or word-by-word reveals.
- Stock music swell under a voice that's fighting it.
- Generic abstract 3D, particles, or gradient blobs standing in for the product.
- A cursor that zooms on everything, so nothing is emphasized.
- UI shown in a tilted 3D device mockup floating over a gradient.
- An obviously synthetic voice with flat stress on the product's name.
- Captions in a different face from the brand, or burned over the UI being shown.
- Feature-list pacing: a new screen every two seconds with no beat held.
- The end card is longer than the hero moment.

## Calibration

Before scoring, each critic answers: of the strips in front of you, which one was made by an agent from a template, and what gave it away? The answer goes in the round log; if the critics name ours, the giveaway is the first finding to fix.
