# Preferences

The user's naming picks and rejections from past runs, with the reason for each. Read before the brief. 

## What the name must say

- **Say what the product does.** A first round of evocative names lost because a stranger couldn't parse what the product does from any of them. Weight the brief toward suggestive names a stranger can parse (the product's category and action visible in the word), the same on-the-nose preference as the brand idea. Run the evocative territories as the wildcard, not the main pool.
