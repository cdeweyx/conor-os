# Treatment

Read at the treatment step, once the structure-locked pass on a surface is approved and the surface is a front door (or the user calls the page boring). visual-assets runs the work; this is how hi-fi frames and receives it.

## Front door or working screen

A results panel is a working screen; a home screen, an empty state, or an onboarding step is a front door, and a plain form on one reads as unfinished however well the tokens are applied. Expect "boring" as the user's first reaction to a plain front door, and open the treatment axis before he has to say it. Working screens skip the treatment.

## The round

It's a round inside the loop, not a pass after it: the same comments-and-pick rhythm, run while the page is still open to change. Call visual-assets, which runs in this order:

1. About ten directions on one axis, with the form untouched.
2. The user's pick or combination.
3. A research lane on the winner's technique when it's outside the toolkit.
4. The make with that technique: generated and seeded, not drawn from memory.
5. The shared critic loop on the layer (hi-fi's scored critic loop, run on the layer in place).
6. The hand-back: one named layer at the back of the artboard, with the seed, the parameters, and any new tokens recorded.

Say which steps of visual-assets ran and which didn't; a treatment that skipped research or the scorecard isn't the skill having run.

## Mixed surfaces and small front doors

- A surface can be a front door in one state and a working screen in another (a search page before and after a query). The treatment applies to the front-door state, and the states step decides which of its elements, if any, carry into the working state as something quiet.
- Empty and error states are front doors at a small scale: a message with a link reads as unfinished, and a stock glyph reads as cheap. They get the same loop shrunk down. Classify the visual first: a generic object is sourced from an icon set through visual-assets' sourcing gate, and only a product-specific one is constructed. Then a short research lane on how peers do it, six to eight directions on one axis in the product's or domain's own vocabulary, the user's pick, and a centered block of glyph, sentence, and one action.
- The first treatment on a product sets the decorative vocabulary. Later surfaces reuse it rather than opening a new axis, and refine and polish checks that they still read as one product.
