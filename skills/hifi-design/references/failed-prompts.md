# Failed prompts

Prompts that produced a bad first pass, appended at the skill pause with one line on what went wrong and the fix that worked. Rerun them on newer models before assuming the fix is still needed; a failure on one model is a probe on the next. Newest at the bottom.

Format: date, surface, the prompt or brief line that carried it, what came back, the fix.

- A card list, first directions round: "three card directions differing in density, same data" over nine unweighted facts. Every direction drew nine equal facts, five ways, and each read as noise. Fix: the metric sidebar (job and size tier per number, the user's yes on the table) before the brief; it took ten minutes and settled it.

## A "nothing" line on three cards

The first pass at a card list rendered "Nothing due" on the three quiet cards. The rule against a line that says nothing on every card was in constraints.md from the previous surface's pause; the pass read preferences.md and not constraints.md, because the skill only named constraints at polish. Fix: constraints.md is read end to end before the first write (SKILL.md, the brief section), and zero-to-one carries the two-halves rule. Rerun on newer models: generate the card list from the brief and check whether the quiet card gets a line.
